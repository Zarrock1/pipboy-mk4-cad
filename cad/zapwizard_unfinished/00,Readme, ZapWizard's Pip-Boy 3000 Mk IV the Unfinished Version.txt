Files:

I have to start of with a word of warning: Do not expect to be able to build a Pip-Boy next week using these files!

The files included here are the raw files from my unfinished Pip-Boy 3000 Mk IV design. These files were released in January 2018. The file set contains just about everything I have created for the project.

The original "Master" CAD files were created in SolidEdge. Do not confuse SolidEdge with the more common SolidWorks.
I have also included STEP CAD files for all custom, electrical and hardware parts. And there there is one massive STEP file that contains the entire assembly. It may be imported it into another CAD program, if that program can handle the huge amount of data. Otherwise there are individual STEP files for each part, but then you are piecing together a puzzle.
I have included STL 3D files for all the custom parts. These are intended as quick reference, do not expect to just print out the STL files on a FDM 3D printer and then piece together the whole thing. No guarantees are given for the accuracy or printability of the files. Finally, I have included the raw artwork files.

If you can't print the parts yourself, then you can purchase some of the 3D parts from my store on Shapeways.
Visit: www.3DPipBoy.com to order.

The design:

If you have read the thread on the RPF, then you know how long this project has been running. I started the design just after the game as announced and had much of the inital design completed before it launched. Much of the project has changed over time, and that there is much left to complete. The design was only ever intended to have a minimum amount of compromises.

At the time of this writing here was the planned set of features:
-3.81" High resolution AMOLED screen.
-Android or Linux OS, based on Dragonboard 410c
-All knobs and dials fully functional and wired into the microcontroller.
-Backlit buttons, RAD gauge and Radio gauge.
-Movable RED gauge needle.
-Geared down radio dial.
-Four way plus select D-pad.
-Detachable USB cable with retracting reel mechanism.
-Ejecting Holotape cassette mechanism.
-Functional optical Holotape with selectable tape identifier.
-Latching closure.
-Custom stand with charger cable built-in.
-Microphone and speaker.
-PIN Diode radiation detector.
-Heartbeat monitor, via bluetooth to a seperate smart watch.

Here are the items I never completed:
-A good way to secure it to your arm. (I was looking at hacking a GoPro wrist mount)
-Built-in heart rate monitor. (Integrated into the wrist mount).

General limitations of this design:
-This was designed to fit tightly around my own arm. It doesn't scale well, so if you need it larger, you will have to work hard to alter the design.
-The 1Ah battery may not last for a whole day of Cosplay. But you can hide an external power cable inside a arm cuff or something similar.
