3D, Macro Knob:

Part Notes:
This part is designed to be made from 3D printed SLS nylon.
You can order this part at: www.3DPipBoy.com

This part is designed to fit into one of the Potentiometers, and be secured using a C-Clip.
The holes in the part mate with a spring-ball to provide a solid snap-action.
The large opening is designed to mate with a metal pin to provent over-rotation.
