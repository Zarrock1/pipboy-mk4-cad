3D, Reel:

Part Notes:
This part is designed to be made from 3D printed SLS nylon.
You can order this part at: www.3DPipBoy.com

This part is designed to hold a thin 3.0mm flexible USB cable. It will hold about 12 inches of cable.
The cable should be loosely wound to prevent friction during re-winding.

I never really solved the spring tension on this part. So it is hit or miss when it works. Perhaps
greasing it may help.

The middle is meant to hold onto a slip-ring PCB.
