Part Notes:
This part is designed to be made from 3D printed SLS nylon.
You can order this part at: www.3DPipBoy.com

This is the single most complex part in the design. It was made a singular part both to make it very
strong and light, as well as to lower the cost compared to ordering a lot of sub-components.

This part requires complex painting, and multiple finishes. All the holes should be sized correclty
when printed in SLS nylon. Each hold has an excape to prevent clogging of powder during the cleanup
process. All the mounting points for the various hardware is built-in to the model.
