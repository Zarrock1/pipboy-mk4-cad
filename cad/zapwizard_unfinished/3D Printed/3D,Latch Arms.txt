3D, Latch Arm 1 and 2:

Part Notes:
This part is designed to be made from 3D printed SLS nylon.
You can order this part at: www.3DPipBoy.com

These parts can be metalized with electro-less plating, rub-and-buff or spray paint.
The goal is to simulate a cast aluminum part.

This part could also be cast in metal with the side holes drilled through.
The dimesions of the cast part would need to be adjusted for shinkage.

The part does need to be somewhat flexible in order to allow the locking action to take place.
