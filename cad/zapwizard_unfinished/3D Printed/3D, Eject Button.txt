3D, Eject Button:

Part Notes:
This part is designed to be made from 3D printed SLS nylon.
You can order this part at: www.3DPipBoy.com

The part can be metalized with electro-less plating, rub-and-buff or spray paint.
Since this part doesn't have compound curves, you can also try a aluminum or mylar foil tape.
The goal is to simulate a cast aluminum part.

This part could also be cast in metal with the side holes drilled through. The dimesions of the cast part would need to be adjusted for shinkage.
