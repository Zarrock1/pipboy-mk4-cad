3D, Stand Base:

Part Notes:
This part is designed to be made from 3D printed SLS nylon.
You can order this part at: www.3DPipBoy.com

These was designed to emulate the look of the stand included with the "Pip-Boy Edition" of the game.

The back has a hold for a charger cable to be added. The charger cable can be routed up and into the
power connector hidden in the inside of the Pip-Boy.

The front is flat to allow an adhesive label to be added.
