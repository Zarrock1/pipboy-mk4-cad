Metal, Label Stand:

Part Notes:
Real aluminum labels are made by photo-exposing aluminum sheet with a resist on the surface.
The resulting plate is very durable and sharp. This technique has been used for decades.
Most high quality label shops can make these, but require a minimum sheet size and order size.

It is also possible to make it by laser-etching anodized aluminum.

The vector artwork for this file is in the Artwork folder.

The part is intended to be stuck to the base using adhesive. 3M 300LSE is the best to use for this.
