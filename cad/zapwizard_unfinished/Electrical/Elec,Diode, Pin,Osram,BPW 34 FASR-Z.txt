A PIN Diode can work as a radiation detector. The larger the PIN diode interface, the more sensative
the detector. You can wrap the whole detector with very thin foil tape to block outside interference.

There are many schematics out ther for PIN diode radiation dectors, however in the end I would
suggest purchasing a pre-made detector module such as this: 
https://www.sparkfun.com/products/14209
