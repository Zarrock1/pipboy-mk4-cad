Notes for Elec,Guage Needle Motor,Simco,2022-716

MFG: Simco
PN: 2022-716
URL: http://www.simcoaftermarket.com/specialty-oem/micro-air-core/

This is the smallest real guage motor I could find on the market. Note that Simco is not in the
habbit of selling one or two units, so if you call them, ask nicely and know what your asking for.
I recommend purchasing the dampened version so you don't have to eat battery power holding the needle.

These don't work like normal motors, servoes or anything like it. Instead they have two coils which
hold the needle at a paticular angle. You will need a special motor driver (see their datasheets)
as well as doing some math in the processor.
