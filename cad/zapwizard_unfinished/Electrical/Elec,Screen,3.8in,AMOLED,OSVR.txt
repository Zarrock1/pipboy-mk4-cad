Notes for Elec,Screen,3.8in,AMOLED,OSVR

Okay, this was the hardest part to find and source. But due to the innovations in VR headsets, you
might be able to get your hands on one.

The display is from the opensource OSVR virtual reality headset developed by Razer:
https://github.com/OSVR/OSVR-Docs/blob/master/README.md
https://osvr.github.io/using/

The Dragonboard 410C can drive a MIPI display such as this. And it is one reason it was selected.
You can look through the design of the OSVR for schematics on driving the display.
